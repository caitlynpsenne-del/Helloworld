About Me

Im currently a student Botswana Accountancy College studying Business Intelligence and Data Analytics.Im a member of the school book club, cooordinated and in charge of managing any and every role of the book club committee members.

My book obsession

Books have always been more than just pages with words to me. They are experiences, emotions, and lessons wrapped into stories and ideas. I especially enjoy two types of books: fictional stories and psychological books. Even though they seem different, both shape the way I think, feel, and understand the worldFictional books feed my imagination and emotions. When I read fiction, I step into other lives, other worlds, and other possibilities. I get to feel what characters feel — their love, fear, excitement, and pain. Through stories, I travel to places I have never been and experience situations I may never face in real life.On the other hand, psychological books feed my mind in a different way. They help me understand how people think, why they behave the way they do, and how emotions influence decisions. These books make me more aware of myself and others. I learn about topics like habits, motivation, personality, mental health, and relationships.

What makes me the person I am

I'm naturally a deep thinker and a bit of a daydreamer. My mind is always exploring — whether it's imaginary worlds from fiction or the inner workings of human thoughts and emotions. I tend to reflect a lot, notice small details, and read between the lines, both in stories and in real life. I'm the kind of person who connects deeply with characters, analyzes motivations, and wonders why people feel and act the way they do. Curiosity about the human mind and imagination are both big parts of who I am.

My Values

I believe stories and psychology share a common purpose: helping us understand ourselves and each other better. I value empathy, emotional awareness, and personal growth. I think learning never really stops, especially when it comes to understanding feelings, behavior, and the different perspectives people carry. Kindness, open-mindedness, and self-reflection are principles I try to live by, and they shape how I see the world.
